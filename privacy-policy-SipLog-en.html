Privacy Policy

SipLog Water Tracker · Last updated: 2026-11-05

This policy explains how SipLog Water Tracker handles your information. In short: it does not collect any.

What we collect

SipLog Water Tracker collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash reporting service.

Data stored on your device

The app stores daily water intake and your goal setting locally so the app works the way you expect. This information never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Network access

The app works fully offline. It does not send any request to our servers or to third parties.

Third parties

We do not share, sell or transfer any information, because we never receive any.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
